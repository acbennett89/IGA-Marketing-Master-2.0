INSURANCE GLOSSARY — terms, acronyms, and conventions you (Claude) will encounter in broker-supplied PDFs. Use this when disambiguating ACORD-form labels, EPIC vocabulary, and common LOB shorthand. Each entry below is a candidate label or value you may see in PDFs — match liberally to the canonical concept.

================================================================================
LINES OF BUSINESS (LOB)
================================================================================

GL / CGL — General Liability / Commercial General Liability. Protects against bodily injury and property damage claims arising from business operations. Look for "Each Occurrence", "Aggregate", "Products/Completed Ops", "Personal & Advertising Injury".

BAP / CA — Business Auto Policy / Commercial Auto. Covers vehicles owned/leased/hired/non-owned by the business. Look for "Combined Single Limit (CSL)", "Bodily Injury", "Property Damage", "Uninsured/Underinsured Motorist (UM/UIM)", "Medical Payments", "Hired Auto", "Non-Owned Auto".

WC / WORK COMP — Workers' Compensation. Required in most states for employee injuries. Look for "Each Accident", "Each Employee", "Policy Limit", "Class Code", "Premium Basis", "Estimated Annual Payroll", "Experience Mod / E-Mod / EMR".

UMB / UMBRELLA — Umbrella / Excess Liability. Sits over GL/BAP/EL. Look for "Each Occurrence", "Aggregate", "Self-Insured Retention (SIR)", "Underlying Limits".

PROPERTY / COMMERCIAL PROPERTY — Building, Business Personal Property (BPP), Business Income (BI). Look for "Building Limit", "Contents Limit", "BPP", "Business Income", "Coinsurance %", "Replacement Cost (RC)", "Actual Cash Value (ACV)", "Deductible".

IM / INLAND MARINE — Movable property. Tools, equipment, contractors equipment, builders' risk. Look for "Scheduled", "Unscheduled", "Blanket Limit".

BUILDERS RISK / BR — Course-of-construction property coverage.

CYBER / CYBER LIABILITY — Network security, data breach, ransomware. Look for "First-Party", "Third-Party", "Privacy Liability", "Network Security", "Cyber Extortion".

EBL / EPLI — Employee Benefits Liability / Employment Practices Liability. EPLI covers wrongful termination, harassment, discrimination claims. EBL covers errors in benefits administration.

D&O / DIRECTORS & OFFICERS — Coverage for board/officer decisions.

E&O / PROFESSIONAL — Errors & Omissions / Professional Liability. Look for "Claims-Made", "Retroactive Date", "Prior Acts".

CRIME / FIDELITY — Employee theft, forgery, computer fraud.

POLLUTION / EIL — Environmental Impairment Liability / Pollution Liability.

COMMERCIAL PACKAGE / BOP — Business Owners Policy bundles GL + Property for small businesses. Treat each line separately when extracting.

================================================================================
PARTY ROLES
================================================================================

NAMED INSURED — the legal entity (or person) the policy is written for. Often listed as "First Named Insured" if multiple. Always extract this. EPIC field: account.named_insured.

DBA / D/B/A — "doing business as". A trade name distinct from the legal entity. Often appears below the named insured.

ADDITIONAL INSURED (AI) — third party added to the policy via endorsement (e.g., a landlord, a contractor's general contractor). Repeatable group: additional_insured.

LOSS PAYEE / LIENHOLDER — entity entitled to claim payments (typically a lender on financed equipment/vehicles). Repeatable group: loss_payee.

CERTIFICATE HOLDER — recipient of an ACORD 25/27/28. Often the same as an AI but not always.

INSURER / CARRIER — the insurance company writing the policy.

PRODUCER / AGENCY — the broker/agency. Look for "Producer:", "Agency:", "Broker:".

UNDERWRITER — individual at the carrier responsible for the account.

PRIOR CARRIER — the previous insurance company. Repeatable group: prior_carrier.

================================================================================
LIMITS, DEDUCTIBLES, AND PREMIUM TERMS
================================================================================

EACH OCCURRENCE / PER OCCURRENCE — the most the carrier pays for a single claim event.

AGGREGATE / GENERAL AGGREGATE — the most the carrier pays in a policy term across all claims.

PRODUCTS-COMPLETED OPERATIONS AGGREGATE — separate aggregate for products/completed-ops claims.

PERSONAL & ADVERTISING INJURY (P&A) — non-bodily-injury torts: libel, slander, false arrest, etc.

CSL / COMBINED SINGLE LIMIT — single limit covering both BI and PD on auto policies.

SPLIT LIMIT — separate BI per person / BI per accident / PD per accident on auto policies (e.g., "100/300/100").

DEDUCTIBLE — operator's out-of-pocket per claim.

SIR / SELF-INSURED RETENTION — like a deductible but the insured handles the claim until the SIR is exhausted (umbrella convention).

PREMIUM — what the insured pays. Annual unless otherwise noted.

MINIMUM PREMIUM — the floor regardless of audit.

DEPOSIT PREMIUM — initial premium subject to audit.

EARNED / UNEARNED PREMIUM — pro-rata of premium based on policy term elapsed.

PREMIUM BASIS — the exposure used to compute premium (payroll, sales/receipts, square footage, units, vehicles, etc.).

CLASS CODE — NCCI/ISO classification for the operation. WC class codes are 4-digit (e.g., "8810" for clerical office). GL class codes are 5-digit ISO.

EXPERIENCE MOD / E-MOD / EMR — Experience Modifier on WC policies (1.00 is industry average; lower is better).

================================================================================
DATES AND POLICY TERMS
================================================================================

EFFECTIVE DATE — when coverage starts. Often labeled "Effective" or "Eff Date" or "Policy Period From".

EXPIRATION DATE — when coverage ends. Often "Exp Date" or "To" on a policy period range.

POLICY PERIOD — the date range coverage is in force.

RETROACTIVE DATE — earliest date a covered claim can have arisen (claims-made coverage).

PRIOR ACTS COVERAGE — extends retro date back further than policy inception.

EXTENDED REPORTING PERIOD (ERP) / TAIL — period after expiration during which late-reported claims are still covered (claims-made).

================================================================================
DOCUMENT TYPES YOU WILL SEE
================================================================================

DECLARATIONS PAGE / DEC PAGE — the cover sheet of a policy summarizing limits, premium, named insured, and policy period. The most data-dense document.

ACORD 125 — Commercial Insurance Application (general info).

ACORD 126 — Commercial General Liability section.

ACORD 127 — Business Auto section.

ACORD 130 — Workers' Compensation section.

ACORD 140 — Property section.

ACORD 25 / 27 / 28 — Certificates of Insurance (Liability / Property / Auto).

LOSS RUN — claim history report from a prior carrier. Each row is a loss/claim. Repeatable group: loss.

SUPPLEMENTAL APPLICATION — LOB-specific or industry-specific extra questions (e.g., "Roofing Supplemental", "Cyber Supplemental").

SCHEDULE OF VEHICLES — list of covered autos with year/make/model/VIN/garaging.

SCHEDULE OF DRIVERS — drivers with license number / DOB / years experience.

SCHEDULE OF LOCATIONS — covered premises addresses.

SCHEDULE OF HAZARDS — for GL, the operations and class codes.

SOV / STATEMENT OF VALUES — building/contents values for property policies.

================================================================================
COMMON PDF QUIRKS
================================================================================

- Hand-written values on a typed form: extract verbatim, set needs_review=true.
- A label appears but the value is in a separate column on the next page: extract with the label's page; note the cross-page issue in source_quote.
- A field has multiple values across the document (e.g., named insured spelled differently in two places): extract both occurrences if they differ; the merge layer will surface a conflict.
- Limits expressed as "$1,000,000 / $2,000,000": typically per-occurrence / aggregate; emit each as its own field.
- Limits expressed as "100/300/100" on auto: BI per person / BI per accident / PD per accident, in thousands; emit each as its own field.
- "Included" or "Incl" in a limit cell: the value is "included" — emit value="included" (string), confidence appropriate.
- Premium with "Min" or "MP" suffix: minimum premium applies; capture and note in source_quote.
- "TBD" / "TBA" / "Pending": value=null, needs_review=true, source_quote captures the placeholder.
